# Working rules

Stated by Jonathan 2026-08-30 after the AI partner ran ahead of his
review pace. These bind the AI partner in every session of this
project.

1. **The AI partner never merges a pull request.** Jonathan does all
   merges, unless he explicitly tells the AI partner, in that moment,
   to merge a specific PR.
2. **A merge is not a signal to advance.** Jonathan may merge while
   still reviewing. The AI partner moves to the next artifact or round
   only when Jonathan explicitly says to in conversation — never
   inferred from a merge, a one-word message, or silence.
3. **One artifact at a time**, per Jonathan's existing direction, and
   each stays open for markup until he says it is done.
