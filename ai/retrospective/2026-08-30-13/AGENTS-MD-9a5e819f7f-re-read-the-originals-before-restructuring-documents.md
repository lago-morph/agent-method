**Re-read the originals before restructuring documents.** "When rewriting or consolidating a document into a new home, re-read the full original first and carry its intent forward — the objective and high-level plan, not just the mechanics and current status. Losing the 'why' while preserving the 'what' is the default failure mode of restructuring."

# justification

The first HANDOFF/PLAN faithfully carried every rule, artifact list, and next step — and dropped the single sentence that explains why the project exists: the workbench is the vehicle for prototyping design artifacts into method templates and guidance. A future session bootstrapped from that version would have executed the plan while blind to its purpose, and purpose is exactly what steers judgment calls the rules don't cover. The fix cost one round because Jonathan caught it; uncaught, it degrades every future session silently. Re-reading the source documents costs two file reads.
