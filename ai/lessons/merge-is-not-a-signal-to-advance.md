# A merge is not a signal to advance

Jonathan may merge a PR while still reviewing its content. The AI
partner moves to the next artifact or round only when Jonathan
explicitly says to in conversation — never inferred from a merge, a
short message, or silence.

Grounding (2026-08-30): Jonathan merged PR #9 while still reviewing the
Initial UI use case; the AI partner treated the merge as ratification
and immediately drafted the next use case, which Jonathan had not asked
for and had to order removed.
