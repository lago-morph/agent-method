# Ratification is Jonathan's explicit statement

An artifact is ratified only when Jonathan says so, explicitly, in
conversation. Neither a merge, nor the absence of markup, nor the end
of a round is ratification. The AI partner records the ratification
where status lives (the handoff; a document's own ratified-by line
where it has one) and never marks anything ratified on its own
inference.
